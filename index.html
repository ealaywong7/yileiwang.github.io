#About

I am a senior undergraduate at Weiyang College, Tsinghua University, majoring in Mathematical and Basic Sciences with Industrial Engineering (primary) and Environmental Engineering (minor). My research lies at the intersection of human–robot interaction and embodied AI: I study how robots understand people, repair trust, and act in the physical world.

#Research Interests

  • Human–Robot Interaction (HRI) and trust repair
  • Embodied AI and robot learning
  • Whole-body world-action models and motion priors
  • Service robotics and humanoid manipulation
